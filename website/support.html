FAQs

  • I can’t sign in: Check your internet connection and try again. For email sign‑in, verify your address and password. For Apple/Google, ensure those accounts are active on your device.
  • Deleted items reappeared: Make sure you’re online when deleting; if it persists, contact us with the time of the action and steps to reproduce.
  • No confirmation email: Check spam/junk and allow messages from our domain.

Account & Data

  • Export data: Use in‑app options (as available) or email us for assistance.
  • Delete account: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your data.
