Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2025

Pocket Balance ("we", "our") helps you plan your finances. We collect only the information needed to run the app and never sell your data.

Data We Collect

  • Account data: email, name, optionally phone for sign‑in.
  • Budget data you create: income, expenses, subscriptions, categories.
  • Diagnostics: basic logs/metadata to improve reliability (no tracking).

How We Use Data

  • Authenticate you and keep your session active.
  • Store and display your budgeting data in the app.
  • Maintain and improve app reliability.

Sign‑in & Storage

We use Supabase to provide authentication and secure storage. Your data is stored in Supabase in the region chosen for our project.

Payments

Pocket Balance is a paid app with a one‑time purchase processed by Apple. We do not process or store your payment card details.

Analytics & Tracking

No third‑party advertising or cross‑app tracking. We do not build marketing profiles.

Data Retention

Your data remains until you delete it in the app or delete your account. Deletion removes your data from our databases (subject to backup and legal obligations).

Your Rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data. Contact us if you need help.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Contact

Email: support@pocketbalance.com

Changes
